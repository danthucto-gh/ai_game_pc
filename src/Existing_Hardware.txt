1.2) Explanation of the Sticker

1.2.1) Part Number (GV-N3090GAMING OC-24GD):
- GV: Gigabyte Video (graphics card division).
- N3090: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 (GA102-300-A1 die).   
- GAMING OC: Gaming Series, factory overclocked (1755 MHz boost clock vs. 1695 MHz reference).   
- 24GD: 24GB of GDDR6X VRAM across a 384-bit bus (936.2 GB/s bandwidth).   
1.2.2) Revision (rev. 1.0):
Indicates the first PCB and WINDFORCE 3X cooler revision. Because NVIDIA never introduced a Lite Hash Rate (LHR) limiter on the RTX 3090, Gigabyte kept this card on rev. 1.0 throughout its entire production run.   
1.2.3) Serial Number (SN214441046047):
- 21: Manufactured in the year 2021.
- 44: Manufactured in Week 44 of 2021 (November 1–7, 2021). Being a late-2021 build is a strong plus: by this batch, Gigabyte had already resolved the recessed 8-pin power connector pin issue that affected early autumn-2020 batches.
- 41046047: Factory line and sequential unit ID.
1.2.3) EAN-13 Barcode (4 719331 307547):
- 471: GS1 country prefix for Taiwan.
- 9331: Manufacturer code registered to Giga-Byte Technology Co., Ltd.
- 30754: Specific product identifier for the GV-N3090GAMING OC-24GD.   
- 7: Modulo-10 check digit, confirming your barcode transcription is mathematically flawless.
